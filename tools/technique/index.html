Weekly sheets

Public speaking techniques

One technique a week, from Aristotle to today: the idea, how to do it, a drill and your homework.

Connecting with your audience

Ethos — Why should they listen to you?
Earn the room's trust in the first thirty seconds — before you ask it to believe anything.Pathos — Make them feel it, not just know it
People act on what they feel. Give your point a face, a moment and a stake.

Building speeches

Logos — A point they can repeat tomorrow
One clear claim, backed by reasons the audience already accepts.The Five Canons — Invention, Arrangement, Style, Memory, Delivery
The oldest checklist for preparing a talk — and still the best.Problem – Solution – Impact — The structure that sells an idea
Make them feel the problem, show the way out, then paint the world after.Story – Message – Action — Tell it, mean it, ask for it
A story earns attention; a message gives it meaning; an action makes it count.Hooks — The first ten seconds
Never open with your name and the agenda. Open with a reason to listen.

Mastering delivery

The Pause — Silence is part of the speech
A two-second pause reads as confidence; an 'um' reads as doubt.Vocal Variety — Pace, pitch, volume — and contrast
Monotone isn't a voice problem, it's a contrast problem.Eye Contact & Space — One person, one thought
Talk to faces, not to the room — and move only when the idea moves.

Special situations

Improvisation — Speaking without a script
You don't need more ideas — you need a structure you can grab in three seconds.Pitching Yourself — Who you are, in sixty seconds
Not your job title — the problem you solve and the proof you've solved it.
