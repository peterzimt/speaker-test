People join by scanning the QR code and typing their name: they're added to the speakers list. You can change who films any speech in its row.

🤖 AI analysis

The filming phone sends the sound of each filmed speech. Tick this for speeches nobody films. After the speech, the speaker's card shows the exact transcript, the counts (“um”s, pace, pauses, hedges…) and coaching feedback that includes what the room said.

🎥 Why film? Watching yourself is the fastest way to improve: you see the habits the room noticed — the pacing, the “um”s, the hands — next to their scores. The video is unlisted and only the speaker gets the link. Untick for anyone who prefers not.

Tip: open voting when a speaker starts, close it a minute after they finish, then show the results and let them scan their personal card.
