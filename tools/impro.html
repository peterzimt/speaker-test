How to use it in a session

Project it, spin, and invite the next volunteer. They get the think time on the clock, then speak until the bar runs out — amber at 30 seconds left, red at time. The structure gives them a spine; the constraint stretches one specific skill. Lock any reel to keep it for the whole round, so everyone practises the same structure on different topics.

Want to practise it with real people? Join the weekly meetup in Madrid, or find your speaker profile first.
